# Homepage contact design

## Goal

Give hiring managers and engineers a specific reason to contact Tejas without making the section sound like a sales call to action.

## Content

- Heading: Let's talk.
- Body: Have an interesting engineering problem, a useful product idea, or a role with real ownership? Send me a note.
- Action: `nafdetejas@gmail.com`

Keep email as the only action. GitHub, LinkedIn, and the résumé already appear earlier on the page.

## Presentation

Keep the current typography, spacing, and email treatment. This is a copy-only change.

## Verification

- Test the exact approved heading and body.
- Test that the section contains one email action and no profile links.
- Run the production build.
- Inspect desktop and mobile rendering.
