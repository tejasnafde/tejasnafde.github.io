# Homepage experience design

## Goal

Show Tejas's progression at GeoIQ and provide enough concrete evidence for both hiring managers and engineers.

## Structure

Use one GeoIQ company entry for Jan 2025 to Present. Show two roles in a compact timeline:

1. Software Development Engineer, Jun 2025 to Present
2. Software Development Engineer Intern, Jan 2025 to Jun 2025

Below the timeline, show nine outcomes in three labeled groups. Use a three-column hairline grid on desktop and one column on mobile.

## Evidence groups

### AI and developer systems

- WhatsApp analytics assistant rebuild and production access-control fix.
- Model evaluation suite with about 88% lower daily spend and about 3x faster responses.
- RCA agent that reduced issue resolution from days to hours.

### Data and product platforms

- BigQuery-to-Postgres Airflow pipelines with 94-96% faster full-mart refreshes.
- Payments, invoicing, subscriptions, and expiry workflows.
- Conditional caching with more than 80% lower report-generation latency.

### Automation and operations

- Survey and geospatial presentation pipeline that generates decks in 30 seconds to 5 minutes.
- Real-time messaging used by three production applications.
- Slack, notification, and telephony systems with approvals and delivery tracking.

## Visual behavior

Use the existing studio-technical system. Dates and group labels use the mono label style. Hairlines define the timeline and evidence groups. Do not add cards, logos, illustrations, or motion.

## Verification

- Test that one company entry contains both roles in the correct order.
- Test all three evidence groups and their approved metrics.
- Test that the section maps the role timeline and evidence grid.
- Run the production build.
- Inspect the section at 1440px and 375px in light and dark themes.
