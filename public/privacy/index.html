Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-03

tn07 is the publisher name for a set of personal software projects: Switchboard, Someday, Scout, Follymarket, WatchWithMi and a few smaller tools. They are built and maintained by one person. There is no advertising, and no data is sold. Each app is described below, because they collect different things.

The short version

  • Switchboard runs on your own machines. Your chats, code and terminal output never reach a server operated by tn07.
  • Someday, Scout and Follymarket are hosted apps. They store your account (name, email, profile picture) and the content you add, so that the app works across devices and with the people you share it with.
  • Google sign-in in the hosted apps asks only for your basic profile (openid, email, profile). No app reads your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar.

Switchboard

The desktop app reports five anonymous events: it launched, an agent session started, the feature tour was completed or skipped, and a process crashed. Each report carries the event name, the app version, the operating system (macOS or Windows), the chip (arm64 or x64), and a random install id that is generated on your machine and is not linked to you, your account, your hostname, or your files. A session report names only the agent kind (Claude Code, Codex, or OpenCode). No message content, file path, project name, terminal output, or error text is ever sent. Turn the reports off at any time in Settings > General > Privacy; the app stops sending immediately. Development builds never send anything.

The Switchboard mobile app can sign in with Google. When it does, it requests these OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/cloud-platform - used solely to open Google Cloud IAP tunnels to Compute Engine instances you already have access to, so the app can reach your own development machines without a VPN. The app does not read, modify, or store any other Google Cloud resource, and it does not enumerate your projects, billing, or storage.
  • openid and email - used solely to display which account is signed in.

These tokens are stored in the operating system keychain on your device (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). They are sent only to Google, and never to a server operated by tn07.

Someday

Someday stores your account (email, display name, profile picture), the circles you create or join, and what you save in them: links, titles, notes, tags, reactions, and the photos and notes you attach when you mark something done. Members of a circle can see what is saved in it. Data is stored in a Supabase Postgres database. If you turn on notifications, a push token is stored so that the app can notify you; pushes go through Expo's push service and the browser's web push service.

Scout

Scout stores your account, your job preferences, the jobs it finds for you, and the resume and voice notes you upload. Account data and jobs are stored in Google Cloud Firestore; resumes and voice notes are stored in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket. To score jobs and tailor resumes, Scout sends your preferences, your resume text and job descriptions to Google's Gemini models on Vertex AI. When you first set up Scout, your name, email, the text you wrote about what you want, and your voice note are also sent to a private Discord channel that only the developer reads, so that a new signup can be checked by hand.

Follymarket

Follymarket is an invite-only play-money prediction market. It stores your account, the markets you create, your positions and your play-money ledger in Google Cloud Firestore. No real money is involved. Signups, new markets, large moves and resolutions are posted to a private Discord channel for the group. If you allow notifications, a web push subscription is stored.

WatchWithMi and the smaller tools

WatchWithMi has no accounts. A room exists only in memory while people are in it, and it keeps only the display name you type. Searches use the YouTube Data API. Other tools on this domain are used by their developer, or by one hiring team that signs in with its company Google accounts.

Usage counts and page views

The hosted apps and websites send anonymous event counts (for example, "an item was saved") to analytics.tn07.dev, a Cloudflare Worker, and Cloudflare Analytics Engine keeps them for three months. They name the app, the event and the platform, never the content. The websites also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies. Both are used only to see whether people use a feature and whether a release breaks.

What is not collected

  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other sites.
  • No contact list or location.
  • No access to any Google data beyond your basic profile, except the Switchboard tunnel scope described above.

Deleting your data

Email nafdetejas@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, name the app, and your account and content in it are deleted within 30 days. To remove an app's Google access, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. Signing out of an app deletes its tokens from your device.

Third parties

Data is processed by the services that run these apps, each under its own privacy policy: Google Cloud (sign-in, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Run, Vertex AI and IAP), Supabase (sign-in and database), Cloudflare (hosting and analytics), Vercel (hosting), Expo (push notifications) and Discord (the private developer channels described above). Nothing is shared with anyone else, and nothing is sold. Source for most projects is on GitHub.
