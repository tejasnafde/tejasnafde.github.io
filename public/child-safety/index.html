Child safety standards

Last updated 2026-10-03

These standards apply to every app published as tn07, including Someday. tn07 has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises a minor, including images, video, drawings and text.
  • Grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or any attempt to contact a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material.

Our apps are for adults aged 18 and over. Accounts found to belong to a minor are removed.

How to report

In Someday, use Settings > Report a concern, or email nafdetejas@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety report". Include the circle or content involved if you can. Reports are read within 24 hours.

What we do

  • Remove the content and the accounts involved as soon as a report is confirmed.
  • Report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant national authorities, including India's National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), as the law requires.
  • Keep the evidence that the law requires us to keep, and cooperate with law enforcement.

Child safety point of contact

Tejas Nafde, nafdetejas@gmail.com. See also the privacy policy.
